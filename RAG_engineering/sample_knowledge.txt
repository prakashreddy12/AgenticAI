Password Reset
- To reset your password, visit portal.example/reset.
- Use your company username and current MFA code.
- If you have trouble logging in, contact the support desk.

VPN Troubleshooting
- For VPN issues, reconnect to the VPN and re-enter your company password.
- If the certificate is invalid, update the VPN client and retry.
- If the issue continues, open a help desk ticket with your device ID.
